# EasySewa – On-Demand Home Services App (UI/UX Design)

**Smart Services at Your Door — Quick · Reliable · Professional**

EasySewa is a mobile app UI/UX design for an on-demand home and personal care services platform, in the style of Urban Company. It was designed in **Figma** and covers the full journey from login to booking and tracking a service, for both the **customer** and the **service provider**.

> This is a design project. The repository contains exported screens and a prototype walkthrough video, not a working app.

## 🎬 Demo

Prototype walkthrough: [`demo/easysewa-demo.mp4`](demo/easysewa-demo.mp4)

## ✨ Features

- **Phone login with OTP** – mobile number entry (+91) and a 4-digit verification code with resend timer.
- **Service categories** – Beauty Services: Massage Therapy, Hair Styling, Nail Art & Care, Makeup, Facial Care.
- **Service selection** – itemised services with prices (e.g. Classic Straight Hair ₹350, Bob cut ₹450).
- **Cart and payment summary** – included items, promo codes (e.g. `TRYBEAUTY`), service fee, total, special instructions, cancellation policy.
- **Address selection** – saved addresses with edit, remove and add-new options, plus a 3-step progress bar (Address → Payment → Review).
- **Disclaimer** – transparent terms before booking confirmation.
- **Nearby provider map** – locate available providers around the user and make a request.
- **Booking history (user)** – upcoming booking with OTP, call, chat and track; past services with ratings and *Book again*.
- **Booked services (provider)** – assigned job with start, call, chat, go-to-location and cancel; completed services list.

## 🎨 Design Approach

- **Consistent visual language** – a green palette (leaf-inspired logo), rounded cards and buttons, and clear typography.
- **Clear hierarchy** – prices, service names and primary actions stand out on every screen.
- **Two-sided flow** – separate views for customers and service providers.
- **Trust and transparency** – cancellation policy, price disclaimer and OTP-based service start.
